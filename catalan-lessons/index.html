Useful Catalan

Feel more at home in Barcelona and Catalonia.

Catalan can open doors to local culture, community, work, and everyday conversation.

  • Everyday interactions: greetings, cafés, shops, neighbors, and social plans.
  • Life in Catalonia: appointments, schools, local services, and community events.
  • Conversation: speak more comfortably and follow what people are saying around you.
  • Grammar and pronunciation: understand the patterns and sounds that make Catalan distinctive.
  • Language choice: learn when Catalan is used and how it fits alongside Spanish.

Spanish or Catalan?

You don’t have to decide alone.

If you’re new to Barcelona and don’t yet speak Spanish, Spanish is often the broadest place to start. If you already speak Spanish—or Catalan matters to your family, work, studies, or community—it may make sense to begin Catalan now.

I teach both languages and can help you choose a practical order based on your circumstances.

Catalan lesson FAQs

Good to know before you begin.

Can I learn Catalan if I don’t speak Spanish?

Yes. Catalan is its own language, and you can learn it directly. We’ll decide whether it’s the most practical first step for your situation.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. We can begin with pronunciation, greetings, and useful everyday conversations.

Are the lessons only for people in Barcelona?

No. Lessons are online, so you can learn from anywhere. The content is especially relevant to people living in or connected to Catalonia.
