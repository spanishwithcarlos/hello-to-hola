Meet Carlos

A multilingual teacher who knows what learning a language feels like.

I was born in the United States, and I’m a native speaker of English and Spanish. I’ve lived in Barcelona since 2011 and hold C2 proficiency in both Catalan and French.

I hold a BA in English Literature from UCLA and an MFA in Writing from the University of San Francisco. My work has always centered on language—how it’s shaped, translated, and used to communicate meaning across cultures.

Alongside teaching, I work as a professional translator and editor, helping individuals and organizations move between English, Spanish, and Catalan with precision and clarity.

I’m also a published, award-winning writer working in English and Spanish. I write fiction, nonfiction, and poetry. That literary practice informs my teaching: I pay close attention to nuance, tone, and the choices that make language feel natural rather than mechanical.

I also learned Catalan after moving to Barcelona. I know what it’s like to build confidence in a language while trying to find your place in a new city.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about learning Spanish and Catalan online.

Can I begin before moving to Spain?

Yes. Starting before you arrive can help you prepare for introductions, transport, shopping, housing, and other everyday situations. Because the lessons are online, you can continue with the same teacher after you move.

How much Spanish do I need to live in Spain?

There’s no compulsory level, but even basic Spanish can make daily life easier and help you feel more independent. Your lessons will focus on the situations most relevant to you.

Should I learn Spanish or Catalan in Barcelona?

Spanish is useful throughout Spain and is usually the best place to start if you don’t already speak it. Catalan can help you connect more deeply with life, culture, and community in Catalonia. I can help you decide which language best suits your circumstances.

Do you offer private and small-group lessons?

Yes. You can book private lessons or learn with a partner, friends, or family members who have similar levels and goals.

What happens during the free lesson?

The free introductory lesson lasts 30 minutes. We’ll discuss your experience, goals, and priorities, try some personalized activities, and identify a useful path forward. There’s no obligation to book further lessons.
