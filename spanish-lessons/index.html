Spanish lesson FAQs

Good to know before you begin.

Do I need any Spanish to start?

No. Beginners are welcome, and we’ll start with useful language you can put into practice right away.

Which variety of Spanish will I learn?

Lessons are designed around living and communicating in Spain. We’ll also discuss regional and international differences when they’re useful to you.

Are lessons only for people already in Spain?

No. You can start from anywhere in the world and continue online if you move.
